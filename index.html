Welcome to ChemistNate

Welcome to ChemistNate—a comprehensive, zero-fluff set of online chemistry lecture notes, problem walkthroughs, and cheat sheets designed for high school and university chemistry students.

Modelled after traditional academic reference sites, these notes emphasize clear conceptual breakdowns, step-by-step math applications (such as stoichiometric calculations and equilibrium expressions like $$K_{eq} = \frac{[\text{C}]^c[\text{D}]^d}{[\text{A}]^a[\text{B}]^b}$$), and extensive practice problem sets without unnecessary distractions.

Note to Students: Every chapter features dedicated notes sections alongside structured practice problems. Use the left-hand navigation sidebar to jump straight to the topic or sub-section you are currently reviewing.
