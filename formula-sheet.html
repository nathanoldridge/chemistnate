Stoichiometry

EquationUse
$$n = \frac{m}{M}$$Moles from mass (M = molar mass)
$$\text{Percent yield} = \frac{\text{actual yield}}{\text{theoretical yield}} \times 100\%$$Reaction efficiency
$$M_1V_1 = M_2V_2$$Dilution
$$\text{Average mass} = \sum (\text{fraction} \times \text{isotope mass})$$Average atomic mass

Gases

EquationUse
$$PV = nRT$$Ideal gas law
$$\frac{P_1V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2V_2}{T_2}$$Combined gas law
$$P_{\text{total}} = P_1 + P_2 + \cdots$$Dalton's law of partial pressures
$$\frac{\text{rate}_1}{\text{rate}_2} = \sqrt{\frac{M_2}{M_1}}$$Graham's law of effusion

Thermochemistry and Thermodynamics

EquationUse
$$q = mc\Delta T$$Heat from a temperature change
$$\Delta H^\circ_{rxn} = \sum n\,\Delta H^\circ_f(\text{products}) - \sum n\,\Delta H^\circ_f(\text{reactants})$$Standard enthalpy of reaction
$$\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$$Gibbs free energy (spontaneity)
$$\Delta G^\circ = -RT \ln K$$Free energy and the equilibrium constant

Kinetics

EquationUse
$$\text{rate} = k[\text{A}]^m[\text{B}]^n$$Rate law (orders from experiment)
$$t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{k}$$Half-life, first-order reactions
$$k = Ae^{-E_a/RT}$$Arrhenius equation
$$\ln\frac{k_2}{k_1} = -\frac{E_a}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2} - \frac{1}{T_1}\right)$$Rate constants at two temperatures

Equilibrium

EquationUse
$$K_{eq} = \frac{[\text{C}]^c[\text{D}]^d}{[\text{A}]^a[\text{B}]^b}$$Equilibrium constant
$$K_{sp} = [\text{cation}]^a[\text{anion}]^b$$Solubility product (solids omitted)
$$\Delta G^\circ = -RT \ln K$$Free energy and K

Acids and Bases

EquationUse
$$\text{pH} = -\log[\text{H}_3\text{O}^+] \qquad \text{pOH} = -\log[\text{OH}^-]$$pH and pOH
$$\text{pH} + \text{pOH} = 14.00 \quad (25\,^\circ\text{C})$$pH–pOH relation
$$K_a \cdot K_b = K_w$$Conjugate acid–base pairs
$$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log\frac{[\text{A}^-]}{[\text{HA}]}$$Henderson–Hasselbalch (buffers)

Electrochemistry

EquationUse
$$E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cathode}} - E^\circ_{\text{anode}}$$Standard cell potential
$$\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ$$Free energy from cell potential
$$E = E^\circ - \frac{0.0592}{n}\log Q \quad (25\,^\circ\text{C})$$Nernst equation

Solutions

EquationUse
$$M = \frac{n_{\text{solute}}}{V_{\text{solution (L)}}} \qquad m = \frac{n_{\text{solute}}}{\text{kg solvent}}$$Molarity and molality
$$\Delta T_f = iK_f m \qquad \Delta T_b = iK_b m \qquad \pi = iMRT$$Colligative properties
$$C = k_H P$$Henry's law (gas solubility)
$$P = X \cdot P^\circ$$Raoult's law (vapour pressure)
